Use a vibrant, premium, and elegant look.

Choose colors, symbols, and decorations that authentically belong to the festival named in the topic.

The medium is already specified above — follow it precisely rather

Favor bright, festive colors over consistently dark luxury styling.

Keep the composition clean, modern, and visually engaging.

AVOID THE GENERIC "AI FESTIVAL POSTER" LOOK

A huge number of businesses now generate festival posters with AI, and
almost all of them converge on the exact same look: a hyper-glossy,
symmetrical, front-facing gold idol/statue centered on a pedestal,
surrounded by marigold garlands, floating diyas, and a soft glowing
aura. Do not default to this. Specifically avoid:

- A deity or idol shown as a glossy CGI-perfect statue, centered and
  facing the camera dead-on
- Walls or arches made entirely of marigold garlands
- Diyas or brass items arranged in a perfectly symmetrical row
- An artificial-looking soft golden glow/bloom around the subject
- A composition that would be interchangeable with any other
  business's festival poster if the logo were swapped out

The goal is a poster that is unmistakably this brand's, not a
template anyone could have generated.

AVOID LOOKING "AI-GENERATED"

Do not render this as a digital painting, concept art, or illustrated
fantasy-art scene — visible brushwork, painterly blending, and
dreamlike soft-focus detail are themselves an obvious tell of AI
generation, not a way to avoid it. This should look like it was
either photographed for real, or laid out by a professional graphic
designer using clean, deliberate shapes — not painted by a machine.
Keep faces, hands, and jewelry anatomically consistent and precise,
not stylized or slightly melted the way generative art often renders
fine detail.

IMPORTANT

Do NOT include:
- Company logo
- Company name
- Phone number
- Website
- Footer
- QR code
- Social handles
- Watermarks

Leave the top-left and bottom-right areas naturally less busy for branding that will be added later.
